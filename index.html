04 / Good to know

Curious?
Perfect.

Does this site need a paid website builder?

No. This is ordinary web code. A small static website like this can often be hosted on a free tier.

Can this turn into a much bigger website?

Absolutely. We can add pages, galleries, games, tools, and eventually a database or user accounts if the project calls for them.

Is anything I click here saving my data?

No information is sent to a server by this demo. Your preferred light or dark theme is stored only in your browser's local storage.

Is this already a complete business website?

Not yet! It's a working front-end demonstration. Real forms, payments, login systems, and content management would be added and tested separately.
